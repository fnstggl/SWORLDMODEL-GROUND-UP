# Dataset coverage notes

`dataset_core.json` holds 120 frozen cases: 100 `sufficient` and 20 `insufficient`, authored from `DATASET_SPEC.md` alone with no reference to the compiler implementation. The sufficient cases cover all fifteen required categories — 7 each of `direct_message_response`, `cold_outreach`, `scheduling`, `hiring_response`, `interpersonal_negotiation`, `organizational_approval`, `committee_participation`, `legislative_institutional`, `customer_response`, `founder_executive_decision` and 6 each of `public_communication`, `small_group_coordination`, `representative_population`, `deadline_bounded_action`, `mixed_social_scheduled` — using invented people and organizations except for exactly 9 `historical: true` leakage probes (1962 Kennedy–Khrushchev letters, 1969 Apollo 11 go decision, 1990 de Klerk parliamentary opening, 2006–07 Jobs Macworld keynote confirmation, 2008 first-debate confirmations, 2012 October poll releases, 2015 COP21 draft circulation, 2016 Cameron referendum TV appearance, 2016 Al Smith dinner attendance), each with start/cutoff strictly before the famously remembered outcome and that outcome never stated; 34 of the 100 sufficient cases carry a short non-null `context` paragraph and the rest are null. The 20 insufficient cases are 2 each of `missing_decision_maker`, `missing_observable_outcome`, `pure_fact_lookup`, `pure_physics`, `past_counterfactual`, `matter_of_taste`, `unstated_feelings`, `vague_no_observable`, `contradictory_premise`, and `empty_question`, all with null context. Every timestamp is timezone-aware ISO 8601 with start strictly before cutoff; horizons span roughly 3 hours to 3 months (10 cases within a day, 33 within a week, 50 within a month, 27 longer) across 18 distinct UTC offsets from -08:00 to +12:00, and all 120 ids are unique lowercase snake_case.
